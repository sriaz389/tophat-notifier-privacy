# Privacy Policy — Top Hat Question Notifier

**Last updated:** October 27, 2026

## Overview

Top Hat Question Notifier is an independent browser extension that provides desktop notifications when new classroom questions become available on Top Hat.

## Information Processed

The extension reads limited information from an open Top Hat classroom, including question identifiers, titles, and availability status, to detect newly published questions.

This information is processed locally in the user's browser.

## Data Collection and Storage

Top Hat Question Notifier does not collect, transmit, or sell personal information.

The extension does not operate its own servers, use analytics or advertising trackers, or require users to create an additional account.

Question information is temporarily processed in browser memory to detect new questions and reduce duplicate notifications.

## Notifications

The extension displays desktop notifications when new classroom questions are detected. Notifications may contain the title of the question.

The extension does not submit answers or interact with assessments on behalf of users.

## Third-Party Services

Top Hat Question Notifier works with the Top Hat website but is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially associated with Top Hat.

The extension does not transmit classroom question information to an external notification service.

## Data Security

The extension uses browser-provided functionality to detect classroom questions and display notifications.

## Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the extension's functionality or data practices change.

## Contact

For privacy-related questions, users can contact the developer through the contact information provided on the extension's store listing.
